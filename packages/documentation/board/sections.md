# Group ideas with sections

Group sticky notes and other items on a board into titled sections that move together, and hide or lock a section.

A section is a colored, titled area. Anything that sits mostly inside it belongs to it, so you add an item by dragging it in and remove it by dragging it out.

::: info Before you start
- You need **Editor** access. A viewer link can't change sections.
- The **Retro** and **Brainstorm** templates start with sections already in place. See [Templates](/collaborate/templates).
:::

## Create a section

1. Click empty canvas so nothing is selected.
2. Press `Shift+S`, or click **Section** in the tool dock.
3. Drag a rectangle on the canvas, or click for a default size.
4. Type a title and press `Enter`.

To draw a section around items that are already on the board, select them, right-click one, and select **Create section around selection**. To add one at a spot, right-click empty canvas and select **Insert section**.

![A selected green section titled "Ideas" holding four stickies, with the section controls in the top toolbar](/images/board/sections-selected.png)

## Add and remove items

- To add an item, drag it into the section. It belongs once most of it is inside.
- To remove an item, drag it out.
- A section can hold smaller sections.

Items in a section stay selectable on their own. Click one to move or edit it without moving the section.

## Move a section with its contents

Drag the section from an empty spot inside it. Everything inside moves with it, and connectors attached to those items follow.

## Rename or recolor a section

1. Select the section.
2. Click **Rename section** in the top toolbar, or double-click the section. Type a title and press `Enter`.
3. To change the color, pick Gray, Yellow, Green, Blue, Pink, or Purple in the top toolbar.

## Hide the contents of a section

Select the section and click **Hide contents** in the top toolbar. A cover hides everything inside it for everyone on the board, and its title shows **Hidden**. Click **Reveal contents** to show them again.

Hidden contents can't be selected, and they are left out when you [download the board](/board/export-and-import#download-a-board).

## Lock a section

Select the section and click **Lock section** in the top toolbar. A locked section and everything in it can't be selected or moved, and its title shows **Locked**.

To unlock it, right-click inside the section and select **Unlock “Ideas”**, named after your section.

## Jump to a section

Click **Sections** (the list icon) in the top toolbar and pick a section. The board zooms to frame it. Locked and hidden sections show an icon in the list.

![Sections menu open in the top toolbar listing Start, Stop, Continue, and Action items](/images/board/sections-list.png)

## Delete a section

- To delete only the section and keep its contents, select it and press `Delete`.
- To delete the section and everything inside it, right-click it and select **Delete section and contents**.

Press `⌘+Z` / `Ctrl+Z` to undo either one.

## On a phone

Touch and hold a section to open its menu, which has **Rename section**, **Hide contents**, **Lock section**, and **Delete section and contents**. **Section** is under **More tools** (the ⋯ button) in the tool dock.
