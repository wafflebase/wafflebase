# Board

A board is an infinite canvas where you arrange sticky notes, shapes, drawings, and images with your team.

Use it to:

- Brainstorm with sticky notes, then group them into titled sections
- Sketch diagrams with shapes, connectors, and a marker
- Run a retro or workshop with a shared timer, dot voting, and stamps

![Retro board with the top toolbar, the tool dock at the bottom, the minimap, and the Download button](/images/board/index-overview.png)

The board has two toolbars. The **tool dock** along the bottom holds everything you add: select, sticky notes, text, shapes, lines, the marker, sections, images, stamps, and comments. The **top toolbar** holds undo and redo, **Zoom ▾**, **Grid ▾**, **Sections** (the list icon), **Timer** (the stopwatch icon), and **Spotlight me**. When you select something, the top toolbar also shows controls for it.

## Get started

1. In your workspace, click **New**, then select **New board**. A board named **Untitled board** opens. To start from a ready-made layout instead, select **New from template**. See [Templates](/collaborate/templates).
2. Press `S` to add a sticky note, type your idea, and press `Tab` to add the next one. See [Stickies, shapes, and connectors](/board/stickies-shapes-connectors).
3. Click **Share** in the top right to invite your team. See [Share a document](/collaborate/share).

Click the board's title in the header to rename it. Changes save automatically.

## Move around a board

- **Pan**: scroll or swipe with two fingers on a trackpad. Hold `Shift` and scroll to pan sideways. To pan by dragging, hold `Space` and drag, drag with the middle mouse button, or pick the **Hand** tool (`H`) in the dock.
- **Zoom**: hold `⌘` / `Ctrl` and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad. The point under the pointer stays in place.
- **Zoom to a level**: open **Zoom ▾** in the top toolbar and pick **Fit**, 10%, 25%, 50%, 100%, 200%, 400%, or 800%. **Fit** frames everything on the board. Zoom ranges from 10% to 800%.
- **Use the minimap**: the minimap in the bottom-right corner shows the whole board. Drag inside it to move to another area. Click **Map** to hide or show it.
- **Jump to a section**: click **Sections** in the top toolbar and pick a section.
- **Frame everything**: right-click empty canvas and select **Fit to content**, or press `Shift+1`.
- **Find text**: press `⌘+F` / `Ctrl+F`, type, and press `Enter` for the next match or `Shift+Enter` for the previous one. Find searches sticky notes, shapes, text boxes, and connector labels.

A board opens framed on its content. For every zoom and navigation key, see [Keyboard shortcuts](/board/keyboard-shortcuts#navigation).

### Change the grid

Open **Grid ▾** and pick **None**, **Dot grid** (the default), or **Line grid**. **Snap to grid** (on by default) lines up moved and resized items on the grid, even when the grid is hidden. These are your own settings, saved in this browser. Teammates keep theirs.

## What viewers can do

Someone with a viewer link sees the board update live, along with everyone's cursors and the timer. They can pan by dragging, zoom, find text, and download the board. They can't add or change anything, vote, or comment, and they don't see the toolbars.

## On a phone

On a phone, drag empty canvas with one finger to pan and pinch to zoom. Touch and hold empty canvas to open the menu for adding a sticky note or section. The dock keeps **Select**, **Sticky note**, **Text box**, **Shape**, and **Marker**, and moves the other tools into **More tools** (the ⋯ button). The minimap starts hidden. See [Use Wafflebase on a phone](/start/phone).
