# Draw on a board

Sketch on a board with the marker and highlighter, erase strokes, select with a lasso, and point with the laser.

::: info Before you start
- You need **Editor** access. A viewer link can't draw.
- Drawing works on a phone: draw with one finger, and use two fingers to pinch-zoom.
:::

## Draw with the marker or highlighter

1. Click empty canvas so nothing is selected.
2. Press `M` for the marker or `Shift+M` for the highlighter. Or click **Marker** (the pen icon) in the tool dock.
3. To change the pen, color, or width, click the arrow next to **Marker** (**Pen, colour and width**). Pick **Marker** or **Highlighter**, one of eight colors, and **Thin**, **Medium**, or **Thick**.
4. Drag on the canvas to draw. Hold `Shift` while drawing for a straight line.
5. Press `Escape` when you finish.

The pen stays on stroke after stroke until you press `Escape`. The highlighter is wide and see-through, so text beneath it stays readable. Your pen choice is remembered in this browser. Teammates see your stroke while you draw it.

![Pen popover with Marker, Highlighter, eight colors, three widths, Laser pointer, and Smart shapes](/images/board/draw-pen-popover.png)

## Turn sketches into clean shapes

1. Click the arrow next to **Marker** (**Pen, colour and width**).
2. Click **Smart shapes** so it reads **On**.
3. Draw a box, a circle, a straight line, or a line with an arrowhead.

The marker stroke becomes a rectangle, an ellipse, a line, or an arrow. A stroke it can't recognize stays as drawn. Smart shapes work with the marker only, not the highlighter. Pressing `⌘+Z` / `Ctrl+Z` removes the shape; to keep a sketch as drawn, turn **Smart shapes** off before you draw it.

## Erase strokes

1. Press `Shift+E`, or click **Eraser** in the tool dock.
2. Drag across the strokes you want to remove. Each stroke you touch is deleted whole.
3. Press `Escape` when you finish.

The eraser removes only marker and highlighter strokes. To delete anything else, select it and press `Delete`.

## Select with the lasso

Hold `Option` / `Alt` and drag a loop around items on empty canvas. Or click **Lasso select** in the tool dock, then drag. Every item inside the loop is selected.

## Point with the laser

1. Press `Shift+K`, or click the arrow next to **Marker** and select **Laser pointer**.
2. Drag to draw a red trail that everyone on the board sees.

The trail fades after about a second and a half. Nothing is saved to the board. Press `Escape` to turn the laser off.

## On a phone

**Marker** and its arrow stay in the compact tool dock. **Eraser** and **Lasso select** are under **More tools** (the ⋯ button). With the marker on, one finger draws and two fingers pinch to zoom. Tap **Select** to go back to moving items.
