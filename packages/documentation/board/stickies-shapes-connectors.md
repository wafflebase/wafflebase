# Add stickies, shapes, and connectors

Add sticky notes, shapes, text boxes, images, and connectors to a board, then style and arrange them.

::: info Before you start
- You need **Editor** access. A viewer link can't change the board.
- Images: PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP, up to 10 MB each.
- Single-key tools such as `S` and `T` work only when nothing is selected. With an item selected, typing a letter edits that item.
:::

## Add a sticky note

1. Open the board and click empty canvas so nothing is selected.
2. Press `S`, or click **Sticky note** in the tool dock. A yellow sticky note appears in the middle of the screen, ready for typing.
3. Type your text. The text shrinks to fit the note.
4. Press `Escape` to finish.

To pick another color first, click the arrow next to **Sticky note** and pick a color. To add a sticky note at a specific spot, right-click there and select **Insert sticky note**.

## Add the next sticky note quickly

- **While typing**: press `Tab` or `⌘+Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter`. The note is saved, and a new one opens to its right in the same color, size, and text size.
- **With one sticky note selected**: press `⌘+Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter`, or click one of the **+** handles on its four sides to add a note in that direction.

![A selected green sticky note with + handles on all four sides and the sticky controls in the top toolbar](/images/board/stickies-plus-handles.png)

## Turn a pasted list into sticky notes

1. Copy a list of lines, or a range of cells from a spreadsheet.
2. Click empty canvas on the board and press `⌘+V` / `Ctrl+V`.

Each line becomes one sticky note, laid out in a grid. Copied cells keep their rows and columns. One paste creates up to 100 notes.

## Change color, size, and text size

1. Select one or more sticky notes.
2. In the top toolbar, pick one of the six colors: Yellow, Green, Blue, Pink, Orange, or Purple.
3. To resize, open the size menu (**Sticky size**) and select **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, or **Wide**.
4. To change the text size, click **Decrease text size** (−) or **Increase text size** (+).

You can also drag a corner handle. A square note stays square.

### On a phone

The six colors fold into one swatch menu in the top toolbar. Tap it to pick a color.

## Show or hide who wrote a sticky note

Each new sticky note records your name. To show it at the bottom of a note, select the note and click **Show author** (the person icon) in the top toolbar. Click it again to hide the name.

To hide every name on the board, for an anonymous brainstorm, right-click empty canvas and select **Hide sticky authors**. This applies for everyone on the board until someone turns it off.

## Add a shape

1. Click empty canvas so nothing is selected.
2. Click **Shape** in the tool dock and pick a shape: Rectangle, Rounded rectangle, Ellipse, Diamond, Triangle, Parallelogram, Arrow, Double arrow, Star, Speech bubble, Cylinder, or Hexagon. For the full catalog, click **More shapes…**.
3. Click the canvas to place the shape, or drag to set its size.

Press `R` for a rectangle or `O` for an ellipse. To type in a shape, double-click it.

## Add a text box

1. Press `T`, or click **Text box** in the tool dock.
2. Click the canvas, or drag to set the width, and type.

## Add an image

- Click **Insert image** in the tool dock and choose a file. The image lands in the middle of the screen.
- Or paste an image, or drag an image file onto the board.

To crop, select the image and click **Crop image** in the top toolbar. **Reset crop** restores the full image. To replace an image, delete it and insert a new one.

## Connect items

1. Click **Line** in the tool dock and pick **Line**, **Arrow**, **Connector** (right-angle), or **Curved**. Or press `L` for a line, `Shift+L` for an arrow, or `X` for a connector.
2. Drag from one item to another. Each end attaches to the shape you drop it on.

An attached end follows its shape when the shape moves. To add a label, double-click the connector and type.

![Two sticky notes joined by an arrow with the label "blocks" in the middle](/images/board/stickies-connector-label.png)

## Edit text on an item

1. Double-click a sticky note, shape, text box, or connector. Or select it and press `Enter` or `F2`.
2. Use the top toolbar for font, size, bold, italic, underline, text color, alignment, and lists.
3. Click **Done** or press `Escape` when you finish.

## Change fill and border

Select a shape, text box, or connector. In the top toolbar, use **Fill color** for a solid color or a gradient, and the border controls for line color, weight, and dash. A connector has no fill.

## Arrange items

1. Select the items. Drag across empty canvas to select several, or `Shift`-click each one.
2. Click **Arrange** in the top toolbar.
3. Select an option:
   - **Group** or **Ungroup**
   - **Order**: bring to front or forward, send backward or to back
   - **Align**: needs two or more items
   - **Distribute**: needs three or more items
   - **Tidy up**: lines up the selection in an even grid (`Option+Shift+T` / `Alt+Shift+T`)
   - **Rotate 90° clockwise** or **Rotate 90° counter-clockwise**

Press arrow keys to nudge a selection 1 pixel, or hold `Shift` for 10 pixels.

### On a phone

**Arrange** moves to the start of the top toolbar while something is selected. Touch and hold an item to open its menu.

## Select similar items

Right-click an item and select **Select all with same colour**, **Select all stickies**, or **Select all of this type**. Then restyle or move them together.

## Known limitations

- A board has no tables.
- Freehand drawing uses the marker, not the **Line** menu. See [Draw](/board/draw).
