# Comment and mention people

Leave feedback on part of a document without changing it, and use `@` to bring a teammate into the discussion.

::: info Before you start
- Comments work in sheets, docs, boards, and PDFs. Presentations and notes don't have comments.
- To comment, sign in and have edit access: a workspace member, or someone on an editor [share link](/collaborate/share). Viewer links can read comments but not add them.
- You can mention only members of the document's workspace.
:::

## Comment on a cell

1. Open the sheet and select the cell.
2. Right-click and select **Insert comment**, or press `⌘+Option+M` / `Ctrl+Alt+M`.
3. Type your comment and click **Comment**.

The thread opens when you select the cell again.

## Comment on text in a doc

1. Open the doc and select the text you want to discuss.
2. Right-click and select **Insert comment**, or press `⌘+Option+M` / `Ctrl+Alt+M`.
3. Type your comment and click **Comment**.

The text is highlighted for everyone. Click the highlight to open the thread. A comment needs selected text; with only a blinking cursor, nothing happens.

![Doc with two highlighted comments and the Comments panel open on the Open tab, one thread resolved](/images/collaborate/comments-doc-panel.png)

## Comment on a board

1. Open the board and click **Comment** in the bottom tool dock.
2. Click an item or an empty spot to drop a pin.
3. Type your comment and click **Comment**.

To pin a comment to a selected item, press `⌘+Option+M` / `Ctrl+Alt+M`. See [Facilitate a session](/board/facilitate-a-session).

## Comment on a PDF

1. Open the PDF and select some text.
2. Click the **Comment** button that appears next to the selection.
3. Type your comment and click **Comment**.

For scans and figures, click the **Comment on a region** icon in the header and drag a box over the page instead. See [PDFs](/files/pdfs).

## Mention someone

Mentions work in comments on sheets, docs, and boards.

1. In a comment, type `@`.
2. Keep typing to filter the list of workspace members.
3. Press `↑` or `↓` to choose a person, then `Enter` or `Tab`.

The mention appears as a blue chip, and that person gets a notification when you post. To post any comment from the keyboard, press `⌘+Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter`.

![Comment box with "@ha" typed and the member list suggesting hackerwins](/images/collaborate/comments-mention.png)

## Reply, resolve, and reopen a thread

- **Reply**: open the thread, type in the reply box, and click **Reply**.
- **Resolve**: click the check icon (**Resolve**) on the first comment. The thread moves to the **Resolved** tab.
- **Reopen**: open the thread from the **Resolved** tab and click the check icon (**Reopen**).

## Edit or delete your comment

1. Hover your comment and click **⋯** (**More actions**).
2. Select **Edit**, change the text, and click **Save**. Or select **Delete**.

You can edit or delete only your own comments. Deleting the first comment in a thread deletes the whole thread.

## Browse all comments

1. Click the comments icon (**Show comments**) in the header. In sheets and docs you can also press `⌘+Option+Shift+M` / `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M`.
2. Choose the **Open** or **Resolved** tab.
3. Click a thread to jump to its cell, text, or pin.

In a doc, if someone deletes the text a comment was on, the thread moves to **Orphaned** on the **Open** tab so the discussion isn't lost.

### On a phone

The comments icon moves into the header's **⋮** menu (**More**). Tap **⋮**, then **Comments**, to open the panel and read, reply, and resolve.

## Get notified

The bell in the header shows how many unread notifications you have, up to **99+**.

1. Click the bell.
2. Click a notification to open what it's about. It's marked as read.
3. To clear the badge, click **Mark all read**.

You're notified when:

- someone mentions you in a comment
- someone replies to or resolves a thread you commented in
- someone joins a workspace you own, or accepts an invite you created
- a template you published is approved, rejected, removed from the public gallery, or sent back for review
- a template is waiting for review, if your deployment made you a template reviewer

Starting a thread notifies nobody unless it mentions someone. You're never notified about your own actions.

## Known limitations

- People on a share link can't be mentioned and have no bell.
- On a board, comments can't be added through a share link.
- Restoring an older version removes comments added after it. See [Version history](/collaborate/version-history).
- Comments aren't copied when you make a copy or use a template.
