# Delete and restore documents

Deleted documents go to the workspace's Trash, where you can restore them or delete them for good.

Each workspace has its own Trash. Nothing in it is deleted automatically.

::: info Before you start
- To move a document to the Trash, restore it, or delete it forever, you need to have created it, or own the workspace.
- While a document is in the Trash, nobody can open it, and its share links and template stop working. Restoring brings them back.
:::

## Move a document to the Trash

1. On the documents list, click **⋯** at the end of the document's row.
2. Select **Move to Trash**.

A message says *Moved “&lt;title&gt;” to Trash*. Click **Undo** in it within 10 seconds to put the document back.

To trash several documents, select them with the checkboxes and click **Move to Trash** in the bar above the list.

![Documents list with the Moved "Q3 budget" to Trash message and its Undo button](/images/collaborate/trash-undo-toast.png)

## Restore a document

1. In the sidebar, click **Trash**.
2. Click **Restore** in the document's row.

The document goes back to the folder it was in. If that folder was deleted, it goes to the top of the workspace.

![Trash page with a board, a doc, and sheets, the Deleted column, and Restore and Delete forever on each row](/images/collaborate/trash-page.png)

### On a phone

**Restore** and **Delete forever** show as icons only, and the **Deleted** column is hidden. Tap the circular-arrow icon to restore, or the trash icon with an X to delete forever.

## Delete a document forever

::: danger This can't be undone
The document can no longer be opened or restored, and its share links stop working for good.
:::

1. In the sidebar, click **Trash**.
2. Click **Delete forever** in the document's row.
3. In the confirmation, click **Delete forever**.

## Empty the Trash

1. In the sidebar, click **Trash**.
2. Click **Empty trash**.
3. In **Empty trash?**, click **Empty trash**.

Every document you can manage is deleted forever. Documents that other people created stay in the Trash for their author or a workspace owner.

## Known limitations

- Deleted folders don't go to the Trash. Their documents move to the top of the workspace instead. See [Folders](/collaborate/folders#delete-a-folder).
- Deleting a workspace deletes its documents without using the Trash.
- Trashed documents don't appear in search or the documents list until you restore them.
