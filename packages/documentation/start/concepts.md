# Concepts

Wafflebase keeps every document in a workspace, and workspace membership decides who can open it.

**Short answer:** every member of a workspace can open and edit every document in it. Folders only organize. To give someone outside the workspace access to one document, send them a share link.

## Workspaces

A workspace is a shared space for a team's documents. Your first sign-in creates one for you, and you can belong to several. Switch between them with the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, which also has **New workspace**.

Each member has one of two roles:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Member | Open, edit, copy, rename, and comment on every document in the workspace. Create viewer share links. |
| Owner | Everything a member can, plus invite and remove members, change the workspace name and URL, delete the workspace, and manage every document |

A document's author can also manage that document: move it, move it to the Trash, create editor links, and publish it as a template. Owners add people by creating an invite link in **Settings**. See [Workspaces and roles](/collaborate/workspaces).

## Documents

Everything in a workspace is a document. The type decides which editor opens it:

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| Sheet | A spreadsheet with formulas, charts, and pivot tables |
| Document | A paged document for writing |
| Presentation | A slide deck |
| Note | A Markdown note |
| Board | An infinite canvas for sticky notes, shapes, and drawing |
| PDF, image, or file | An uploaded file you view, comment on (PDFs), and download, but don't edit |

Sheets, documents, presentations, notes, and boards save automatically as you type, and several people can edit one at the same time. They also keep a [version history](/collaborate/version-history) you can restore from.

## Folders

Folders organize documents inside a workspace and can nest to any depth. They don't change who can open a document: a member sees every folder. Deleting a folder moves the documents inside it back to the workspace root. See [Folders](/collaborate/folders).

## Share links

A share link gives anyone who has it access to one document, without signing in or joining the workspace.

- A **Viewer** link lets people read the document. The editor shows a **View only** badge.
- An **Editor** link lets people change it. Only the document's author or a workspace owner can create one.
- A link can expire after 1 hour, 8 hours, 24 hours, or 7 days, or never (**No limit**).
- People who open a link without signing in show as **Anonymous** to everyone else.

Revoking a link stops it working at once. See [Share a document](/collaborate/share).

## Templates

A template is a document published so others can start from it. Using a template makes a new copy in your workspace, so later changes on either side don't affect the other. A template can be visible to people with its link, to your workspace, or, after review, to everyone in the public gallery. See [Templates](/collaborate/templates).

## Trash

Moving a document to the Trash hides it everywhere, including its share links. A 10-second **Undo** appears right after. The document's author or a workspace owner can restore it from **Trash** in the sidebar, which brings its share links back, or delete it forever. Documents stay in the Trash until someone deletes them. See [Trash](/collaborate/trash).

## What this means for you

- To keep a document from a teammate, put it in a different workspace. A folder won't hide it.
- To show one document to someone outside your team, send a viewer link rather than inviting them to the workspace.
- If you can't choose **Editor** in the Share dialog, ask the document's author or a workspace owner to create the link.
- If a document or a link vanished, look in the Trash before anything else.
