# Sheets

Sheets is a spreadsheet for formulas, charts, and live data that your team edits together.

Use it to:

- Track a budget, a task list, or an invoice with formulas that update as you type
- Summarize data with charts and pivot tables
- Query a database or read a lakehouse table into a tab

![The Sheets editor with its toolbar, Name box, formula bar, and tab bar labeled](/images/sheets/overview-toolbar.png)

## Get started

1. In your workspace, click **New** and select **New sheet**. A sheet named **New Sheet** opens with one tab, **Sheet1**.
2. Click a cell and type a value or a formula such as `=SUM(B2:B7)`, then press `Enter`. See [Formulas](/sheets/formulas).
3. Click **Share** in the top right to invite your team. See [Share a document](/collaborate/share).

To learn the basics in one sitting, follow [Build a budget](/sheets/build-a-budget).

## On a phone

You can view and edit a sheet on a phone. Double-tap a cell to edit it in a panel at the bottom of the screen, pinch to zoom, and touch and hold a cell for its menu. Tools that don't fit the toolbar, such as number formats, borders, and charts, are in the ⋮ (**More formatting options**) menu. See [Use Wafflebase on a phone](/start/phone).
