# Slides

Slides is the Wafflebase presentation editor, where you build a deck with your team and present it full screen.

Use it to:

- Build a deck from layouts, text, images, shapes, tables, and diagrams
- Give every slide a consistent look with a theme, then fine-tune colors, fonts, and backgrounds
- Add transitions and animations, then present from the browser or download the deck as PowerPoint or PDF

![Slides editor with the slide strip, toolbar, Format/Motion/Background/Theme buttons, canvas, and speaker notes](/images/slides/index-editor-overview.png)

## Find your way around

- **Slide strip** (left): every slide as a thumbnail. Click one to open it, drag to reorder, and right-click for slide actions.
- **Toolbar** (top): **Undo** and **Redo**, **Format painter**, the zoom menu (it reads **Fit** until you change it), **Add slide**, and insert tools. The middle of the toolbar changes with what you select: object controls for a shape or image, text controls while you type.
- **Format**, **Motion**, **Background**, and **Theme** (right end of the toolbar): each opens a panel on the right. When the toolbar runs out of room, such as on a narrow window or while an object is selected, they show as icons only.
- **Canvas** (center): the current slide, with rulers along the top and left.
- **Speaker notes** (below the canvas): private notes for each slide.
- **Slideshow** and **Export** (header): present the deck, or download it.

The zoom menu offers **Fit**, 50%, 75%, 100%, 150%, and 200%. **Fit** sizes the slide to the window.

## Get started

1. In your workspace, click **New > New presentation**. The deck opens on one title slide.
2. Click **Click to add title** and type. To add another slide, click **Add slide** (the + button) in the toolbar.
3. Click **Share** in the header to invite your team. See [Share a document](/collaborate/share).

For a guided first deck, follow [Tutorial: build a deck](/slides/build-a-deck).

## On a phone

On a phone the slide strip moves below the slide and the toolbar collapses into bottom sheets. You can add slides, edit text, insert content, style objects, and present. Speaker notes and slide reordering are not available. See [Use Wafflebase on a phone](/start/phone).

A share-link viewer sees the deck read-only. On a phone, a viewer swipes left or right to change slides.
