# Present a deck

Write speaker notes for each slide, present the deck full screen, and download a copy to share or present elsewhere.

## Write speaker notes

1. Open the slide.
2. Click the **Speaker notes…** box below the canvas and type.

Notes save as you type and stay with their slide. A collaborator's changes to the notes appear as they make them.

To make the notes box taller or shorter, drag the divider above it. Your browser remembers the size.

## Start a slideshow

1. Open the slide you want to start from.
2. Click **Slideshow** in the header. The deck fills the screen.

To start from the first slide, click the arrow next to **Slideshow** (**Present options**) and select **Present from beginning**.

You can also press `⌘+Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter` to present from the current slide, or `⌘+Shift+Enter` / `Ctrl+Shift+Enter` to present from the beginning.

![Slideshow button with the Present options menu open, showing Present from current slide and Present from beginning](/images/slides/present-slideshow-menu.png)

### On a phone

Tap the play button (**Slideshow**) in the header to present from the current slide. To start from the first slide, tap the ⋮ button in the header (**More**) and select **Present from beginning**. Tap or swipe to move between slides.

## Move between slides

Each step forward plays the next **On Click** animation, then moves to the next slide.

| To do this | Keyboard | Mouse or touch |
|---|---|---|
| Go forward | `→`, `Space`, `Page Down`, or `N` | Click, tap the right two-thirds of the screen, or swipe left |
| Go back | `←`, `Backspace`, `Page Up`, or `P` | Tap the left third of the screen, or swipe right |
| Go to the first slide | `Home` | |
| Go to the last slide | `End` | |
| Leave the slideshow | `Escape` | |

The pointer hides after three seconds without movement. Move the mouse to show it again.

## End the slideshow

Press `Escape` at any time to return to the editor. After the last slide, a screen reads **End of slideshow — click or press Esc to exit**. Click it or press `Escape`.

## Download the deck

1. Click **Export** (the download icon) in the header.
2. Select **PowerPoint (.pptx)** or **PDF (.pdf)**.

A progress message appears while the file is prepared, then your browser downloads it. On a phone, the export options are under **Export** in the header's ⋮ (**More**) menu.

PowerPoint files keep your speaker notes. PDF files have one page per slide. See [Import and export](/files/import-export) for what each format keeps.

## Known limitations

- Speaker notes are plain text, with no bold, italic, or lists.
- People who open the deck through a share link have no **Slideshow** or **Export** button.
- There is no presenter view. Speaker notes don't show while you present.
- Speaker notes aren't available on a phone.
- If two people type in the same notes box at once, one person's typing can overwrite the other's.
- Each PDF page is an image of the slide, so its text can't be selected or searched, and notes aren't included.
- A PowerPoint export leaves out charts that came from an imported PowerPoint file, without a warning. Keep the original file if you need them.
