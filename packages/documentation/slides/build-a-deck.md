# Build a deck

Build a three-slide project update deck, then present it full screen. It takes about 10 minutes.

You will create a title slide, apply a theme, add a bulleted content slide, add a picture and a shape, set a transition, and start the slideshow.

::: info Before you start
- You need to be a member of a workspace. See [Workspaces](/collaborate/workspaces).
- Use a computer. The phone editor works differently; see [Use Wafflebase on a phone](/start/phone).
- Have a PNG or JPEG image ready, such as a team photo or a product screenshot.
:::

## 1. Create the presentation

1. In your workspace, click **New > New presentation**.
2. Click **Click to add title** and type `Q2 Roadmap`.
3. Click **Click to add subtitle** and type `Project update for the platform team`.
4. Click an empty part of the slide to finish typing.

The deck opens on one title slide, and the slide strip on the left shows its thumbnail.

## 2. Pick a theme

1. Click **Theme** at the right end of the toolbar. The **Theme** panel opens on the right.
2. On the **Themes** tab, click **Streamline**.
3. Click the close button (×) at the top of the panel.

The whole deck takes the theme's colors and fonts. Your text stays where it was.

## 3. Add a content slide

1. In the toolbar, click the arrow next to **Add slide** (tooltip **Choose a layout**).
2. Select **Title and body**. A new slide opens after the title slide.
3. Click **Click to add title** and type `Q2 goals`.
4. Click **Click to add text** and type three lines, pressing `Enter` after each:

   ```text
   Ship the new onboarding flow
   Cut page load time by 30%
   Launch the partner API beta
   ```

5. Select the three lines and click **Bulleted list** in the toolbar.
6. Click **Done** at the end of the toolbar.

## 4. Add a picture and a shape

1. Click the arrow next to **Add slide** and select **Title only**. Type `Team` as the title, then click an empty part of the slide.
2. Click **Insert image** (the picture icon) and choose your image file. The image appears centered on the slide.
3. Drag the image to the left half of the slide, and drag a corner handle to resize it.
4. Click **Shape** (the shapes icon) and select **Rounded rectangle**.
5. Drag on the right half of the slide to draw the shape, then type `On track` to put a label in it.
6. Click an empty part of the slide, click the shape again, then click **Fill color** and choose an accent color from the theme row.

## 5. Add a transition

1. Click **Motion** at the right end of the toolbar.
2. Under **Transition**, set **Type** to **Fade** and **Speed** to **Medium**.
3. Click **Apply to all slides**.

Every slide now fades in when you present.

## 6. Present the deck

1. Click the first slide in the slide strip.
2. Click **Slideshow** in the header. The deck fills the screen.
3. Press `→` or click to move forward, and `←` to go back.
4. Press `Escape` to return to the editor.

![Finished three-slide Q2 Roadmap deck in the Streamline theme, with the Team slide open](/images/slides/build-a-deck-final.png)

Your deck should now have three slides in the Streamline theme: a title slide, a bulleted goals slide, and a Team slide with a picture and a labeled shape.
