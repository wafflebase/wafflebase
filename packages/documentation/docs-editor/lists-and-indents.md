# Use lists and indents

Make bulleted and numbered lists, nest items under each other, and indent paragraphs.

## Start a bulleted or numbered list

1. Put the cursor in a paragraph, or select several.
2. Click **Bulleted list** or **Numbered list** in the toolbar, or press `⌘+Shift+8` / `Ctrl+Shift+8` (bullets) or `⌘+Shift+7` / `Ctrl+Shift+7` (numbers).

Each paragraph becomes a list item. Click the same button again to turn the items back into paragraphs.

### Type a list instead

At the start of an empty line, type one of these and press `Space`:

| Type | To get |
|---|---|
| `-` or `*` | Bulleted list |
| `1.` | Numbered list |

## Add and end list items

1. Press `Enter` at the end of an item to add the next one.
2. Press `Enter` on an empty item to end the list.

The empty item turns back into a normal paragraph.

## Nest list items

1. Put the cursor in a list item, or select several items.
2. Press `Tab` to move them one level in, or `Shift+Tab` to move them one level out.

A list can be up to nine levels deep. Items nested under the one you move go with it. You can also use **Increase indent** and **Decrease indent** in the toolbar.

## Indent a paragraph

1. Put the cursor in the paragraph, or select several.
2. Click **Increase indent** or **Decrease indent** in the toolbar, or press `⌘+]` / `Ctrl+]` and `⌘+[` / `Ctrl+[`.

`Tab` does nothing in a plain paragraph; use these controls instead.

## Set indents with the ruler

The ruler above the page shows the indents of the paragraph with the cursor. Units are inches or centimeters, depending on your browser's language.

1. Put the cursor in the paragraph. The ruler changes one paragraph at a time.
2. To indent only the first line, drag the downward triangle (▽) at the top of the ruler.
3. To indent the whole paragraph, drag the upward triangle (△) at the bottom of the ruler.

A guide line follows the pointer while you drag. To change page margins on the ruler instead, see [Page layout](/docs-editor/page-layout).

![Ruler with the first-line indent triangle half an inch in from the margin, above a paragraph indented to match](/images/docs-editor/lists-and-indents-ruler.png)

## On a phone

Tap ⋮ (**More formatting options**) at the right end of the toolbar. Under **List** you'll find **Numbered list**, **Bulleted list**, **Decrease indent**, and **Increase indent**. The ruler is hidden on a phone, and in any window too narrow to show the full page width, so first-line indents need a wider screen.

## Known limitations

- Each list uses its default bullet and number styles. You can't choose a bullet symbol or a starting number.
