# Find text and check spelling

Search a doc for words or patterns, replace them, and fix misspellings.

## Find text

1. Press `⌘+F` / `Ctrl+F`. The find bar opens.
2. Type what you're looking for. Every match is highlighted, and the bar shows the count, such as **2 of 7**, or **No results**.
3. Press `Enter` for the next match and `Shift+Enter` for the previous one, or click the arrows.
4. Press `Escape`, or click the close button at the end of the bar, to close it.

To narrow the search, turn on **Match case**, or **Use regex** to search with a regular expression such as `20\d\d`.

## Replace text

1. Press `⌘+H` / `Ctrl+H`. The find bar opens with a **Replace** row.
2. Type the text to find, then the replacement.
3. Click **Replace** to change the current match, or **All** to change every match.

People with view-only access can search but don't see the replace row.

![Find bar with a replace row and highlighted matches in the document](/images/docs-editor/find-and-spelling-replace.png)

## Fix a misspelled word

Misspelled words get a red wavy underline as you type.

1. Right-click the underlined word.
2. Select a suggestion at the top of the menu.

The word is replaced, and you can undo it like any edit. While suggestions load, the menu shows **Checking…**; if there are none, it shows **No suggestions**.

![Right-click menu over a misspelled word with spelling suggestions above Paste and Add link](/images/docs-editor/find-and-spelling-suggestions.png)

## Use the right-click menu

Right-click in the text to see the actions that apply where you clicked:

- Spelling suggestions, on an underlined word
- **Cut** and **Copy**, when text is selected, and **Paste**
- **Add link**
- **Insert comment**, when text is selected and you're signed in

Right-clicking in a table opens the table menu instead. See [Tables](/docs-editor/tables).

## On a phone

Find and replace open only from the keyboard shortcuts, so they need a hardware keyboard. Misspelled words are still underlined.

## Known limitations

- Spell check is always on and can't be turned off.
- Words in Latin letters are checked against an English dictionary, so French or German words may be marked. Words in other scripts, such as Korean, are never marked.
- Words in tables, headers, and footers aren't checked.
- With view-only access, the right-click menu shows no spelling suggestions.
