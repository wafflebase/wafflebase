Add content ​

Add slides to a deck and fill them with text, images, shapes, tables, and lines that connect shapes.

Before you start

  • You need edit access. A share-link viewer can page through the deck but can't change it.
  • Most tasks work on a phone; see On a phone for where the controls are.

Add a slide ​

  1. Open the presentation.
  2. Click Add slide (the + button) in the toolbar. A blank slide opens after the current one.

To pick the layout as you add it, click the arrow next to Add slide (tooltip Choose a layout) and select a layout. To add a slide with the same layout as the current one, press ⌘+M / Ctrl+M.

You can also right-click a thumbnail in the slide strip and select New slide.

Duplicate, delete, or reorder slides ​

  1. In the slide strip, click a slide. To pick several, hold Shift and click each one.
  2. Right-click one of the selected slides.
  3. Select Duplicate slide or Delete slide. With several slides selected, the items read Duplicate 3 slides or Delete 3 slides.

To reorder, drag a thumbnail up or down the strip. To duplicate the current slide from the keyboard, press ⌘+Shift+D / Ctrl+Shift+D.

A deck always keeps at least one slide, so you can't delete every slide.

Change a slide's layout ​

  1. Right-click the slide's thumbnail, or an empty part of the slide.
  2. Select Change layout… and pick a layout.

Text you already typed moves into the matching place on the new layout: the title stays a title and body text stays in the body. See Themes and layouts for the list.

Type in a placeholder ​

  1. Click a placeholder such as Click to add title or Click to add text.
  2. Type your text.
  3. Press Escape or click Done at the end of the toolbar.

To edit text that is already there, double-click it. You can also select a text box or shape and start typing, or press Enter or F2.

Add a text box ​

  1. Click Text box (the T icon) in the toolbar.
  2. Click the slide to place a box at the default size, or drag to draw the box you want.
  3. Type your text.

Format text ​

  1. Double-click the text to edit it, then select the words you want to change.
  2. Use the toolbar controls:
ControlWhat it does
Font and Font sizeChange the typeface and size. Use − and + to step the size.
Bold, Italic, UnderlineStyle the selected text.
Text colorChange the text color.
Insert link / Remove linkTurn the selection into a link, or remove one.
Clear formattingRemove bold, color, size, and other text styling from the selection.
Text alignmentAlign left, center, right, or justified.
Numbered list / Bulleted listStart or end a list.
Decrease indent / Increase indentChange a list or paragraph's level.

To place text at the top, middle, or bottom of its box, right-click the box and select Align text top, Align text middle, or Align text bottom.

How text fits its box is set in the Format options panel. See Set text fitting.

Insert an image ​

  1. Click Insert image (the picture icon) in the toolbar.
  2. Choose an image file.

The image appears centered on the slide at its natural size. A large image is scaled down to fit within 80% of the slide's width and height. You can also paste an image with ⌘+V / Ctrl+V, or drag an image file from your computer onto the slide.

To swap the picture and keep its position, select it and click Replace image in the toolbar. To trim it, see Crop an image.

Insert a shape ​

  1. Click Shape (the shapes icon) in the toolbar.
  2. Select a shape. The picker has more than 130 shapes in eight groups: Shapes, Block arrows, Banners, Flowchart, Callouts, Equation, Stars, and Action buttons.
  3. Click the slide to place the shape at its default size, or drag to draw it.

Hold Shift while you drag to keep the proportions, such as a perfect circle or square. To put text in the shape, select it and start typing.

Some shapes show a yellow diamond handle when selected. Drag it to change the shape's form, such as the corner radius of a rounded rectangle or the head of an arrow.

Add a table ​

  1. Click Insert table (the grid icon) in the toolbar.
  2. Point at the grid to choose the number of rows and columns, up to 10 × 10.
  3. Click. The table appears centered on the slide and stays selected.

Double-click a cell to type in it. Press Tab to move to the next cell and Shift+Tab to go back. Tab in the last cell adds a row.

Add or remove rows and columns ​

  1. Click a cell, or drag across several cells to select a range.
  2. Right-click the selection.
  3. Select an item such as Insert row above, Insert column right, Delete row, or Delete column. With a range selected, the items match its size, such as Insert 2 rows below.

To join cells, select them, right-click, and select Merge cells. Unmerge cells splits them again. An insert or delete item is unavailable when it would cut through a merged cell.

Style cells ​

With cells selected, the toolbar shows Cell fill, Align cell top, Align cell middle, Align cell bottom, Cell borders, and Cell padding.

Connect shapes ​

  1. Click Line (the line icon) in the toolbar and select Line, Arrow, Elbow connector, or Curved connector.
  2. Drag from the first shape to the second. Connection points appear as you near a shape, and the end snaps to one.

A connected line stays attached when you move either shape. Hold Shift while you drag to snap the angle to 15° steps.

To label a line, double-click it and type. The label sits at the middle of the line. To change how the line routes, right-click it and select Straight, Elbow, or Curved.

Draw freehand ​

  1. Click Line and select Scribble.
  2. Drag on the slide to draw.

On a phone ​

The phone toolbar changes with what you are doing:

  • Nothing selected: tap Insert (the + icon) for a sheet with Text box, Image, Shape, Line, and Table.
  • Object selected: tap Format for its fill, border, and image controls, or the trash icon to delete it.
  • Typing: Bold, Italic, and Underline stay in the bar. Tap Text formatting for the font, size, color, alignment, and list controls, and Done to finish.

To type in a text box or shape, double-tap it. An empty placeholder opens for typing with one tap. To open an object's menu, such as Duplicate or Delete, touch and hold it. To add a slide, tap Add slide at the end of the slide strip below the canvas. It adds a blank slide at the end of the deck. You can't duplicate, delete, or reorder slides on a phone.

Known limitations ​

  • Slide text has no strikethrough or highlight color.
  • If two people edit the same text box, table cell, or shape text at once, the person who finishes last overwrites the other's changes. See Edit together.
  • Charts come only from imported PowerPoint files and can't be edited. You can move and resize them.
  • Images inside a group can't be cropped. Ungroup them first.
