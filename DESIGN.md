# Design System: Wafflebase

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Ruled Ledger"**

Wafflebase is a set of ledgers: grids, pages, slides and boards laid out on
precise rules, where the document is the subject and everything around it is
ruling and margin. The editors are dense, exact and quiet. Chrome sits on
neutral zinc, thin borders do the work that shadows would do elsewhere, and
controls stay small (28px toolbar targets, 36px form controls) so the canvas
gets the room.

Warmth is the ledger's binding, not its pages. The Butter & Maple palette
(syrup, butter, cream paper, walnut ink) and the Fraunces display face set
the brand surfaces: the homepage, sign-in, the template gallery and the
sidebar wordmark. Inside the editors that warmth survives only as the syrup
accent on the primary action, focus ring and selection frames, and as the
butter wash on whatever is chosen (the active sidebar item, a pressed toggle).
That split is deliberate, and new work keeps it.

The ruler is the recurring motif: the homepage's word-ruler backdrop, the
docs and slides rulers, the grid itself, and mono uppercase labels set like
tick marks.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two layers on one token source (`@wafflebase/core/tokens`): neutral editor chrome, warm brand surfaces.
- One accent, syrup, carrying every "this is the action" signal.
- Flat by default: borders and tonal steps before shadows.
- Small, exact controls; Google-suite-familiar placement inside the editors.
- Light and dark are paired token for token; neither is an afterthought.

## Colors

A cool, near-colorless working surface with a single warm syrup accent; the
brand layer swaps the zinc for cream paper and walnut ink.

### Primary
- **Maple Syrup** (syrup): the one accent as a *graphic*. Focus rings, selection frames and handles in every editor (the sheets active cell, filter range frame, formula references and object layer, docs image selection and drag guides, slides handles and lasso), chart series 1, the brand illustrations. It is 4.27:1 on white, enough for a 3:1 mark and not for words, so it never carries text. In dark mode it becomes **Bright Syrup** (syrup-bright) so it holds contrast on roast surfaces.
- **Strong Syrup** (syrup-strong): the one accent wherever it carries words, the `primary` token in light mode. Primary button and badge fills (5.32:1 under on-syrup), the brand pill CTA, and `text-primary` (5.53:1 on white, 5.14:1 on cream). Dark mode's `primary` stays syrup-bright under roast text (6.71:1).
- **Deep Syrup** (syrup-deep): the light-mode `primary-hover` (6.58:1 under on-syrup), the active sidebar item's text, and brand chip and footer-heading labels. Dark mode's `primary-hover` is **Pressed Bright Syrup** (syrup-bright-pressed, 5.39:1 under roast). Hover always presses *darker*: a translucent `bg-primary/90` fades toward the page and lightens a light-mode fill, so primary controls use `hover:bg-primary-hover`.

### Secondary
- **Butter** (butter): highlight, never text on light surfaces. The **selected** wash (`bg-selected`, 30% light / 18% dark, with `text-selected-foreground` = syrup-deep / butter at 5.99:1 / 8.81:1) marks every "this one is chosen" state: the sidebar's active item, a pressed `Toggle`, a picked shape or line. It is never the hover, which stays the zinc `accent`/`muted` wash, so on and hovered look different. A lighter butter wash (16–35% by engine and mode) is the cell, text and slide-table range selection in the engines; a whole selected row or column header deepens to a syrup tint. Also brand tag and badge chips and the waffle illustrations.

### Tertiary
- **Berry** (berry / berry-bright): brand-surface error and emphasis (the homepage's ✕ marks and code-sample keywords), and a swatch in the formatting palette and the Wafflebase slide theme. It is the `type-pdf` hue too.
- **Leaf** (leaf / leaf-bright): success signals, such as the homepage's ✓ marks. In the app it is the `success` token (`text-success`; 4.91:1 light, 9.76:1 dark) and the `type-sheet` hue.
- **Terminal hues**: the homepage code sample sits on a roast panel in both themes, so its keywords and flags use the locked `--wb-terminal-keyword` (berry-bright, 6.25:1) and `--wb-terminal-flag` (leaf-bright, 8.80:1), never the theme-flipping `--wb-berry` / `--wb-leaf`, whose light stops are 3.69:1 / 3.65:1 there.

### Data and identity
- **Chart series** (`chart-1`…`chart-5`): syrup, denim, teal, rose, olive gold, each with a lifted dark-mode stop. No purple, none equal to a status token, every series ≥3:1 on `background`. Only the analytics trend chart consumes them today; document charts inside sheets and slides keep their own palettes.
- **Document types** (`type-sheet`, `type-doc`, `type-note`, `type-slides`, `type-pdf`, `type-image`, `type-board`, `type-file`): one identity hue per type for list icons and menus — leaf, denim, cocoa, teal, berry, rose, sticky-note gold, zinc. Nothing purple and nothing in syrup's orange band, each ≥3:1 on `background` in both modes.

### Links
- **Link** (link / link-bright, the `link` token, `text-link`): hyperlink text in the docs and sheets link popovers and the sheets grid's linked cells. The one non-syrup hue in chrome, because a URL has to read as a URL: Google's own pair, 5.37:1 on white and 9.44:1 on the dark background. It underlines on hover rather than changing shade. A link-styled *button* (`variant="link"`) is an action, not a URL, and stays syrup.

### Neutral
- **Zinc Paper** (app-background) and **Zinc Ink** (app-foreground): editor chrome, dialogs and popovers in light mode.
- **Zinc Wash** (app-muted) / **Zinc Sub** (app-muted-foreground): hover fills, secondary text, placeholders.
- **Zinc Rule** (app-border): every hairline in the app: toolbars, panels, cards.
- **Control Edge** (app-input-border / app-input-border-dark, the `input-border` token): the boundary of a form control — `Input`, `Textarea`, the `Select` trigger, a `Switch`'s off track. A hairline is ~1.3:1 and fine between regions; a control's edge tells you where to click, so it clears WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 (3.37:1 light on `background`, 3.60:1 dark, 3.21:1 on a dark popover).
- **Cream** (cream-bg) / **Cream Paper** (cream-paper): brand-surface page and raised paper.
- **Walnut Ink** (walnut-ink) / **Cocoa** (cocoa-sub) / **Oat Rule** (oat-rule): brand-surface text, secondary text and borders.
- **Roast** (roast-bg, roast-paper, roast-sub, roast-rule): the brand layer's dark pair. The app's dark chrome stays on zinc (app-background-dark, app-card-dark, app-muted-dark, app-foreground-dark, app-muted-foreground-dark, app-border-dark).

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Syrup is the only chromatic signal in editor chrome. If two things on a toolbar are syrup, one of them is wrong; the same goes for two primary buttons in one view (workspace settings keeps "Create invite link" primary and steps "Create API key" down to outline beside it). Hyperlink blue is the sole exception, and only on actual URLs. Tooltips are ink (`bg-foreground text-background`), not syrup: a tooltip names a control, it is not the action.

**The Two Ledgers Rule.** Editor and workspace screens use the zinc semantic tokens (`bg-background`, `text-foreground`, `border`). Brand surfaces use the `--wb-*` tokens. Don't mix them on one surface.

**The Canvas Hex Rule.** Brand colors are authored as hex because the Canvas engines assign them straight to `fillStyle`. Add a new brand color to `packages/core/src/tokens/palette.ts`, never inline in an engine.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Fraunces (with ui-serif, Georgia)
**Body Font:** the system UI stack in the app; Inter (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui) on brand surfaces
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo)

**Character:** An old-style serif with optical sizing for the brand's voice, set tight and heavy. A plain sans does the work in the editors, and a mono marks code and tick-mark labels.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, clamp(40px, 6vw, 68px), 1.04, -0.025em): homepage hero only, with an italic syrup-deep second line set in Fraunces' true italic at 500, the one italic face `index.html` loads. `.font-display` and `.font-code` set `font-synthesis-style: none`, so an italic at any other weight renders upright rather than browser-slanted: load the face before using it.
- **Headline** (600, 28px, -0.02em): brand-surface page titles (sign-in, template landing).
- **Wordmark** (500, display cut `opsz` 144, -0.025em): the "Wafflebase" lockup, 19px on brand surfaces and 17px in the sidebar. See Components → Mark.
- **Section title** (600, 18px): settings and workspace-settings section headings, the largest app-chrome text outside an analytics stat figure (24px).
- **Title** (600, 16px, 1): card and dialog titles in the app.
- **Body** (400, 14px): all editor and workspace UI text. Inputs render at 16px on mobile, which prevents iOS zoom-on-focus.
- **Control** (500, 14px): button and toggle labels, one weight step above Body so a control reads as actionable without color.
- **Body (brand)** (400, clamp(17px, 1.4vw, 19px), 1.55): homepage lead paragraphs, max ~560px measure.
- **Title (brand)** (600, 19px, 1.3): homepage feature- and use-case-card titles (the use-case cards in Fraunces); the brand nav and footer wordmarks take the Wordmark style at the same size.
- **Body (brand, small)** (400, 15px, 1.55): template-landing descriptions, feature-list titles, the large pill button.
- **Caption (brand)** (400, 13px): card links, interop captions, template metadata.
- **Label** (500, 12px): badges, small metadata.
- **Label (mono)** (400, 11.5px, 0.1–0.14em, uppercase): footer column headings and the template category chip. Free-form tag chips are sans at 11px. Never a kicker or eyebrow above a heading.
- **Micro** (`text-2xs`, 10px, line height 1.5): count badges, avatar initials, swatch captions. Never body copy, and never a label the user must read to act. Nothing in the app renders smaller.

Section labels inside popovers and panels (Theme / Recent / Standard in the color picker, shape categories, pivot sections) are **Label**, sentence case, muted: not an uppercase tracked kicker. Uppercase belongs to real abbreviations and keyboard keys.

**Brand scale.** Brand surfaces (homepage, sign-in, template gallery and landing) set their own pixel sizes rather than the app ramp: 11, 11.5, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 14.5, 15, 16, 17, 19 and 28px, plus the fluid `clamp(15px,1.2vw,17px)`, `clamp(17px,1.4vw,19px)`, `clamp(28px,3.5vw,40px)` and Display. Reuse one of these before adding a new value; app chrome uses only `text-2xs` / `text-xs` / `text-sm` / `text-base` and the Section title, except the paragraph-style menu, whose items preview the sizes of the styles they apply.

**Loading.** The brand faces (Fraunces, Inter, JetBrains Mono) load only on brand routes (`/`, `/login`, `/templates`, `/t/:id`) through `src/lib/brand-fonts.ts`, inserted from script with `display=swap`, so they never block first paint and a self-hosted editor session makes no third-party font request. App routes draw in the system stack. Inter also loads eagerly with the document font catalog, because built-in slide themes set it.

### Named Rules
**The Serif Is a Signature Rule.** Fraunces never appears inside an editor or on a control. It is the brand's voice, used for wordmarks and brand-surface headings only.

**The Document Owns Its Fonts Rule.** Text inside a sheet, doc or slide uses the document's own font picker (the curated family list and Google Fonts catalog), never the UI stack.

## Layout

The app shell is a collapsible left sidebar, a header row (title, sync chip, presence, share), a single-row toolbar, and the document canvas filling the rest. Editors are dense: toolbars are 36px tall with 28px buttons and 2px gaps, grouped by thin vertical separators (20px high). On coarse pointers, toolbar targets grow to 44px and the toolbar scrolls horizontally rather than wrapping.

Spacing follows a 4px base (`xs` 4, `sm` 8, `md` 12, `lg` 16, `xl` 24). Cards and dialogs pad at 24px; cards put 24px between header, content and footer, dialogs 16px.

Brand surfaces are single-column scrolls centred at ~920px for the hero and wider for feature grids (3 columns at ≥768px, 1 below), with 64–80px vertical section padding.

Responsive behavior branches at 768px. Below it, Slides and Sheets switch to dedicated mobile shells (bottom sheets, FABs) instead of shrinking the desktop toolbar.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. App surfaces separate by a 1px zinc rule and a tonal step (background, then muted, then card), not by lift. Shadows are tiny and appear in three places only: a near-invisible `xs` under buttons and inputs, `sm` under cards, and the larger popover/dialog elevation for things that float above the canvas. The brand layer allows a soft, warm-tinted card shadow, one deeper warm panel shadow for the sign-in card and the homepage's open-source panel, and a pressed-paper inset on its primary button, and nothing deeper.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Hairline lift** (`0 1px 2px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.05)`): buttons and inputs at rest.
- **Card rest** (`0 1px 3px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1), 0 1px 2px -1px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)`): app cards.
- **Popover** (`0 4px 6px -1px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1), 0 2px 4px -2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)`): popovers, dropdown and context menus, find bars.
- **Dialog** (`0 10px 15px -3px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)`): dialogs and floating editor panels (comments, chart and pivot editors).
- **Brand card** (`0 1px 0 rgba(42,30,18,0.04), 0 12px 28px -16px rgba(42,30,18,0.18)`): homepage feature, use-case and interop cards, template gallery and landing cards. Use-case cards lift 2px on hover; feature cards scale to 1.005.
- **Brand panel** (`0 1px 0 rgba(42,30,18,0.04), 0 30px 60px -32px rgba(42,30,18,0.18)`): the homepage open-source panel; the sign-in card takes the same shape at 0.22.
- **Brand primary bevel** (`0 1px 0 var(--wb-syrup-deep), inset 0 1px 0 color-mix(in srgb, white 25%, transparent)`): the pill CTA's pressed-syrup edge.

### Named Rules
**The Rule Before Shadow Rule.** Reach for a 1px border or a tonal step first. A shadow means the element floats above the canvas (menu, popover, dialog, drag ghost), not that it's important.

## Shapes

Corners are barely softened in the app. The radius base is 0.3rem (4.8px) and the scale is ordered `xs` 2px ≤ `sm` 2.4px < `md` 2.8px < `lg` 4.8px < `xl` 8.8px (source: `packages/core/src/tokens/radius.ts`, mirrored in `index.css`). Controls take `md`; cards, dialogs and alert dialogs take `xl`; `xs`/`sm` are for tiny marks (swatches, dialog close hit areas) that should read square-ish but not sharp. The brand layer is rounder on purpose: pill CTAs and tag chips, 16px cards. Borders are always 1px. The waffle grid is the one decorative geometry and appears only as brand illustration.

## Components

### Buttons
Precise and restrained.
- **Shape:** gently squared (2.8px). Sizes are 32px (`sm`), 36px (default), 40px (`lg`) and a 36px square icon button.
- **Primary:** strong-syrup fill (`bg-primary`), warm off-white text, 14px medium, 8px 16px padding; hovers darker to `primary-hover` (deep syrup light, pressed bright syrup dark). Every fill/label pair clears 4.5:1, asserted in `packages/core/test/tokens/contrast.test.ts`.
- **Hover / Focus:** a 3px ring in 50% syrup plus a syrup border on `:focus-visible`. Disabled fades to 50% and ignores the pointer.
- **Outline / Secondary / Ghost / Link:** outline is a zinc-bordered background button; secondary is a zinc-wash fill; ghost shows a wash only on hover; link is syrup text, underlined on hover. Destructive uses the destructive red fill.

### Toolbar buttons (signature)
- **Style:** 28×28 icon (`icon`) or labelled-with-caret (`menu`, 6px side padding), 2.8px corners, transparent at rest, zinc wash on hover.
- **State:** toggled state belongs to `Toggle`, not this component. Groups are divided by 20px vertical separators in 40% muted-foreground (the plain `border` is too faint against the toolbar).
- **Toggle:** the pressed control in the same strips. 36px (32px `sm`, 40px `lg`), 8px side padding, 2.8px corners, 14px medium, transparent at rest; hover is the zinc wash with muted-foreground text; on (`data-state=on` or `aria-pressed`) is the butter **selected** wash with selected-foreground text, so a pressed toggle never reads as a hovered one.
- **Tooltip:** inverted ink (foreground fill, background text), 12px, 6px 12px padding, 2.8px corners, with a matching arrow. It fades and zooms in from 95%.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 36px high, 1px `input-border` edge (≥3:1, see Control Edge), transparent fill (in dark, 30% of the 15%-white `input` token), 2.8px corners, 12px side padding, hairline lift, syrup caret.
- **Focus:** the same 3px 50%-syrup ring and syrup border as buttons.
- **Error / Disabled:** `aria-invalid` turns the border and ring destructive red. Disabled fades to 50%.
- **Textarea:** `components/ui/textarea.tsx`, the same edge, caret, ring and invalid state at `min-h-16` with 8px vertical padding.

### Toasts
- **Style:** Sonner through `components/ui/sonner.tsx`, never the raw `Toaster`. It takes the app's *resolved* theme (so a dark-mode user gets a dark toast, and an explicit light choice on a dark OS gets a light one) and maps Sonner's `--normal-bg` / `--normal-text` / `--normal-border` to the popover surface, foreground and `border`, with `lg` corners: a toast is the same floating surface as a popover.
- **Copy:** an error toast names what failed and what to do next. Show a message from the server only through `userFacingError(error, fallback)`, which passes a sub-500 `HttpError`'s text and otherwise shows the fallback, never a `TypeError` or a stack.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 8.8px.
- **Background:** card token (white light, raised zinc dark).
- **Shadow Strategy:** card rest (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px zinc rule.
- **Internal Padding:** 24px, with 24px gaps between header, content and footer.

### Chips / Badges
- **Style:** 12px medium label, 2px 8px padding, 2.8px corners. Default is syrup on off-white; secondary, outline and destructive variants follow the button roles.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar:** near-white zinc (`sidebar`), with the mark and `.wordmark` at 17px (Fraunces once a brand page has loaded it this session, the `ui-serif, Georgia` fallback otherwise). The active item is a butter wash with deep-syrup text and the hover a neutral wash. It collapses to an icon rail.

### Mark
- **Form:** "Facet": a waffle's 3×3 grid of pockets turned 45°, nine rounded diamonds (5.6 units, 1.4 gap, 1-unit corners) in a 32-unit box. One color, no outline, no container tile: the waffle is the grid, and turning it makes it a mark rather than a spreadsheet icon or an app launcher.
- **Color:** `--wb-syrup`, so it lifts to bright syrup in dark mode. A one-color mark keeps its shape and only changes value, so following the theme is safe; a multi-tone mark would lose its internal contrast doing the same. The favicon does the same with a `prefers-color-scheme` rule inside the SVG. The apple-touch icon is the inverse: cream-paper diamonds on a full-bleed strong-syrup square.
- **Sources:** `WaffleLogo` (`src/app/home/primitives/waffle-logo.tsx`) for the homepage nav and footer, sign-in, and the sidebar (22px); `public/icon.svg` (favicon), `public/apple-touch-icon.png` (180px), and `packages/documentation/public/logo.svg` (docs-site nav and favicon) draw the same geometry. Change one, change all.
- **Wordmark:** `.wordmark` (`src/index.css`): Fraunces 500 at its display cut (`"opsz" 144`), -0.025em. Left to automatic optical sizing, a 17–19px wordmark takes Fraunces' chunky text cut, which reads heavy and dated next to a one-color mark. The docs site sets the same on `.VPNavBarTitle .title`.
- **Lockup:** mark left of the wordmark, 28px beside 19px (22px beside 17px in the sidebar), 8–10px apart.

### Brand pill button (brand surfaces only)
- **Style:** fully rounded, Inter 14–15px medium, the `primary` fill/label pair (strong syrup light, bright syrup dark) with a deep-syrup border and bevel. It hovers darker to `primary-hover` and lifts 1px. The ghost variant is an oat-rule outline on walnut ink.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every color from `@wafflebase/core/tokens` (semantic classes in the app, `--wb-*` on brand surfaces), in both light and dark.
- **Do** keep editor controls at 28px (toolbar) and 36px (forms) on fine pointers, and let them grow to 44px on coarse pointers.
- **Do** use the 3px 50%-syrup focus ring for every focusable control.
- **Do** separate panels with 1px rules and tonal steps before considering a shadow.
- **Do** place and behave like the Google suite in the editors (menus, toolbar order, shortcuts).

### Don't:
- **Don't** use Fraunces (beyond the sidebar wordmark), cream paper or pill buttons inside an editor or workspace screen.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent color in editor chrome; status colors (destructive, warning, success) are for status only, and raw Tailwind hues (`text-red-500`, `bg-zinc-300`) never stand in for them.
- **Don't** hard-code a hex in a component or engine when a token exists.
- **Don't** add decorative shadows or gradients to app chrome.
- **Don't** set a kicker or eyebrow label above a heading, or a row of non-metric "stats" under a hero. The heading carries its own weight.
